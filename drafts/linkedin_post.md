3.85M crvUSD went into the crvUSD peg pools through transactions routed via Yield Basis pools during the 8 November ETH flush. The PegKeepers had nothing to withdraw.

I rebuilt the event block by block from Ethereum logs. Predictions were logged before I touched the data.

crvUSD was already at 0.9979 before the drop. It fell to 0.9942 and was still at 0.9967 six hours later.

All five PegKeepers held zero debt when the flush started.

Most of the flow was BTC sold through the Yield Basis BTC pools, not ETH. The logs cannot say if it was arbitrage or routed sales.

Soft liquidation barely engaged. 50k USD of collateral was converted.

A figure of 4.10M crvUSD sold via Yield Basis circulated after the event. My main measure lands 6% below it. The original source and its definition are still unverified.

One event ranks nothing. It does show where the peg risk sits now.
